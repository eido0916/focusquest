Use case: ui-mockup. Design ONE polished portrait mobile game screen for Focus Quest, a Japanese pixel-art fantasy focus RPG. This is a NEW stage-selection design proposal, using the attached screenshot only as a reference for the established royal navy / fine gold ornament / Japanese serif typography / four-tab bottom navigation. Do not reproduce the long list of every chapter. Full-bleed flat screenshot, approximately 9:16, no phone frame, no surrounding mockup, no annotations.

Core design: a beautiful playable-looking chapter map with progressive discovery. ONE current chapter is the main focus, with precisely FOUR numbered stage nodes connected in sequential order by a winding golden-dotted path over a rich coherent pixel-art meadow map. Background is beautiful crisp square-pixel JRPG illustration: sunny green grassland, small river with wooden bridge, a few trees, mountain horizon and a tiny distant ruined fortress, bright blue sky. Original pixel scenery occupies most of the screen; navy translucent UI plates with thin decorative gold borders float above it. Avoid a huge solid navy sheet covering the art. This must feel like an adventure map rather than a list of rectangular buttons. Keep controls large and typography readable.

Example progress state: Stage 1 cleared, Stage 2 currently available and selected, Stages 3 and 4 locked. Only Stage 2 may be attempted. Stage 1 remains available to replay. Show these states unambiguously using both icons and words, not just color.

Layout top to bottom:
1. Compact ornate navy-and-gold title bar reading "冒険の書", small subtle subtitle "九つの世界を巡る旅". A small map/history icon button on its right.
2. Compact chapter strip reading "第1章　草原" and "1 / 4 CLEAR", slim gold progress rule filled one quarter.
3. Main illustrated map across the middle, approximately 43% of screen height, with exactly four medallion nodes on the winding path: lower left circular node 1 with a green check and small navy label "クリア済み" plus tiny replay-arrow glyph; center-left/lower-center highlighted blue-sapphire and gold node 2 with a little flag and the label "挑戦可能"; upper right slightly fogged node 3 with padlock, number 3 and text "未解放"; near a tiny fortress at upper center a locked crown/boss node 4 with number 4 and label "BOSS". Route must visibly connect 1 to 2 to 3 to 4. Stage 2 has a delicate gold selection ring, not a giant arrow. Beyond the current reachable path put soft pixel mist around locked destinations, not over the entire scene. Future enemies are silhouettes. No added fifth stage.
4. Below the map a compact attractive navy and fine gold selection panel. Main heading "STAGE 2", subtitle "草原", small clear line "推奨 Lv.6　・　3連戦". One large gold-filled action button "挑戦する" on the right/lower part. This is the current selection, not an additional stage node.
5. A small next-chapter teaser card, subdued dark blue pixel silhouette of a ghost ship under mist: "次の目的地　幽霊船" and below "草原を踏破すると解放", a small padlock. It is visibly unavailable, no action button. Do not show chapters 3 through 9 at all.
6. Small restrained text/action "クリア済みの章へ" with book/replay glyph, so replay access is evident.
7. Preserve four fixed navy bottom tabs with gold icons and Japanese labels "ホーム", "キャラクター", "設定", "冒険". Adventure tab selected with a gold upper edge and subtle lighter navy.

Aesthetic: faithful detailed crisp pixel illustration underneath, elegant restrained navy/gold interface matching the attached screenshot. Ornate corners are delicate, not excessively thick. Japanese typography beautifully spaced in warm ivory, gold for hierarchy, readable on navy. Strong spacing, clear hierarchy and charming sense of discovery. All Japanese text exactly as supplied; do not add invented long descriptions. No castle courtyard behind everything, no long nine-chapter list, no real photography, no gradients blurring the pixel-art scenery, no characters obscuring controls.
